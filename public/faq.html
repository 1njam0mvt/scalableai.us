About the word "scalable"

What does "scalable" mean?

Scalable means able to grow or shrink in size, output, or capacity without a loss of performance or a redesign. It's most often used to describe systems, businesses, or technology that can handle increasing demand smoothly.

What is a scalable technology, and what does it mean in business, computing, and software?

A scalable technology is a system built to handle more users, data, or workload as demand grows, without needing to be rebuilt from scratch. In business, a scalable model is one where revenue can increase without a matching increase in cost. In computing and software specifically, scalability usually means an application or infrastructure can add capacity — more servers or resources — to handle growth.

What is another word for scalable, and what does "highly scalable" mean?

Common synonyms include adaptable, expandable, flexible, and extensible. Something described as highly scalable can absorb large or rapid increases in demand — like a sudden surge in users or data — with little or no extra manual work.

Is it spelled "scalable" or "scaleable", and how is it pronounced?

"Scalable" is the standard spelling in both UK and US English; "scaleable" is an older variant that still appears occasionally but is considered nonstandard today. It's pronounced SKAY-luh-bl, with the stress on the first syllable.

What does "scaly" mean, and how is it different from "scalable"?

"Scaly" describes something covered in scales, like a reptile or fish, and is unrelated to "scalable." The two words share a root ("scale") but have completely different meanings.

What does "scalable" mean in other languages, such as Bengali, Hindi, or Portuguese?

The English word "scalable" is translated as মাপযোগ্য (mapjogyo) in Bengali, स्केलेबल or मापनीय (mapniya) in Hindi, and escalável in Portuguese. In each language it carries the same core meaning: able to expand or adapt in size or capacity without a loss of performance.
