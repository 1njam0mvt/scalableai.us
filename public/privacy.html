3. How We Use Information

5. Anonymous Preview Chat

The free preview chat on our homepage does not require an account. It's associated with a random identifier generated and stored in your browser's local storage, not with your name, email, or any account. This data may still be processed by the AI providers described above to generate a response, and may be retained for abuse-prevention and service-improvement purposes for a limited period.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember things like your anonymous preview identifier, message count, and interface preferences (such as theme). This data stays on your device and is used only to make the Service function correctly.
